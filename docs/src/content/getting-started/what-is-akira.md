---
title: What is Akira?
description: A digital card collection system on Discord - collect, trade, and chat about anime with your community.
---

**Akira** is a card collection bot for Discord. People drop cards, grab one with a reaction, and keep them in a binder. A normal drop is three cards. Text commands start with `a` (`adrop`, `av`, `ac`). The [command list](/commands/reference) has every command, parameter, and alias. Bits from grabs, daily, and burns can be spent in the shop or given to the server pool.

When you are ready to play, [Your first card](/getting-started/your-first-card) walks through a [drop](/commands/drop) and [grab](/commands/grab). [Importing from Karuta](/getting-started/importing-from-karuta) is the planned spreadsheet path, and it is not finished. Economy basics live under [Bits](/economy/bits).
