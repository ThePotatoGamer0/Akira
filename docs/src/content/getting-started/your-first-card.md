---
title: Your first card
description: React on a drop, say hi to Akira, and welcome the card into your binder.
---

Text commands start with `a`, so a drop is `adrop` or `ad`. Slash `/drop` does the same thing. Three cards appear as one picture. React with 1️⃣ 2️⃣ or 3️⃣ to claim one while the drop is still live. That reaction is the grab. Later, a shop or server buff can change a drop to one card or four.

## After the grab

- `av` opens the card you just grabbed. `/view` needs the 7 character code.
- Browse what you own with [collection](/commands/collection) (`ac`) or the WebUI grid.
- Try [daily](/commands/daily) for 100 bits and check [cooldowns](/commands/cooldowns) (`acd`) before the next grab.
- [Wishlist](/commands/wishlist) and [lookup](/commands/lookup) are how you hunt a specific character.

Every command, parameter, and alias is on the [command list](/commands/reference).
