---
title: Compensate
description: Claim 100 bits after Akira was down, once for each outage.
command: compensate
---

**/compensate** pays 100 bits when Akira was unreachable for at least 30 minutes. The claim stays open for 48 hours after she is back. Each player claims once per outage.

An update that shuts her down on purpose does not open a claim. Running the command when nothing is owed leaves your bits as they are.

## Parameters

None.

## Aliases

No short alias. `acompensate` works.

## Example usage

```
/compensate
acompensate
```

## Tips

- Tell the group the command if you want them to claim. Akira does not announce it.
- 100 bits is one daily. It does not buy a shop buff on its own.
