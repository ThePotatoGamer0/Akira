---
title: Userinfo
description: Bits, card count, prestige, and milestone numbers for one player.
command: userinfo
shortcuts: ui, whois
---

**/userinfo** replies with the name Akira uses, bits, how many cards they own, prestige level, milestones claimed, milestone progress, and the daily bonus.

## Parameters

| Name | Required | What it is |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `user` | No | A mention. Defaults to you. |

## Aliases

`ui` and `whois`. `aui` and `awhois` work.

## Example usage

```
/userinfo
aui <@123>
```
