title: Drop channel in the WebUI
description: A read-only BETA view of the Discord drop channel, on the normal website.
---

**Channel** is marked BETA. It is on the normal website, after you sign in with your username. The Discord activity does not show this page.

The page stays still. The chat fills the space under the nav, opens at the latest message, and scrolls on its own. Scroll up and earlier messages load in. New messages arrive on their own. Reactions are only there to look at. Grabs and new messages happen in Discord.
