description: A read-only BETA view of the Discord drop channel, on the normal website.

**Channel** is marked BETA. It is on the normal website, after you sign in with your username. The Discord activity does not show this page.

Akira reads the server's drop channel and draws the recent messages: names, mentions, embeds, pictures, GIFs, and reactions. The reactions are only there to look at. Grabs and new messages happen in Discord.

Refresh asks her to read the channel again.
