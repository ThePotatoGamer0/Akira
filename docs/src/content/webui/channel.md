title: Drop channel in the WebUI
description: The Discord drop channel on the normal website, including sending messages and using grabs, buttons, and menus as yourself.
---

**Channel** is marked BETA. It is on the normal website, after you sign in with your username. The Discord activity does not show this page.

The page stays still. The chat fills the space under the nav, opens at the latest message, and scrolls on its own. Scroll up and earlier messages load in. New messages arrive on their own.

The bar at the bottom posts into that same Discord channel. Akira sends it with your name and picture, then reads it the way she reads a message you typed in Discord, so `adrop` and the other commands work from here. Enter sends. Shift+Enter adds a line.

Clicking a card reaction grabs that card as you. Akira does not add the reaction herself. Buttons and menus run the same action they do in Discord, and the channel updates for everyone. A note that would only be visible to you in Discord shows on this page.

Akira needs Manage Webhooks in the drop channel for those posts. @everyone and @here stay off on website posts. Those pings follow Akira's permission.
