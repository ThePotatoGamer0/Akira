When you are [signed in](/webui/authentication), **Settings** holds the choices you rarely change. Search, the order for this visit, tags, and select stay on [Collection](/webui/collection).

## General

**What should Akira call you?** is one word, up to 15 letters. Akira capitalises the first letter. She uses it in the home greeting and in `/ping` and `/help`. Leave it blank to use your Discord handle.

**Button buzz** is on at first. This phone gives a short buzz when you press a button or pick something from a menu. The switch is saved on this device.

## Collection

- **Your frame** is the frame for cards that do not have one of their own. Server frame follows the server. A card you frame yourself keeps that choice.
- **Default order** is how the grid opens. The order menu on the collection page can change it while you look, and the next visit starts from this again.
- **Continuous scrolling** is off at first. The collection uses page buttons, 32 cards a page, and each page fills in 16 cards at a time. Turn it on to drop the page buttons. Cards still arrive 16 at a time as you scroll.

## Reminders

Daily, Drop, and Grab are the same switches as **/reminders**. When one is on, Akira DMs you and leaves a note in the bell at the top of the WebUI. Turning it off stops both.
