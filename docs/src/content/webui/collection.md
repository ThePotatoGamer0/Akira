When you are [signed in](/webui/authentication), your cards live under **Collection**. The word **Your** has a small arrow. Open it to look at someone else's collection. Their cards are read-only.

## Getting there

1. Open the WebUI link your admin shared (and finish sign-in if asked).
2. On **Home**, click **Collection** - same kind of link as **Economy** and **Stats**.

## What you see

- Your cards appear as a **grid of thumbnails**. Search by name, series, tag, or card code, and change the order from the menu beside the search box. That menu lasts for this visit. The order the grid opens in is set in [Settings](/webui/settings). **Most recent** puts the latest grab or the latest card you were given first.
- The grid arrives **16 cards at a time**. Page buttons step through the rest, 32 cards a page. Continuous scrolling in Settings removes those buttons and keeps loading 16 at a time.
- The picture is the framed card. A card with its own frame keeps that one. The rest use your frame from Settings when you have set one, and the server frame otherwise. The first time a card is opened, Akira draws that image, so it can take a moment. Later opens use the saved picture.
- **Click** a card to focus it. From there you can change its frame, set its tag, or **burn** it. The character name opens that character on Characters. A holder on a [character](/webui/characters) page opens that print here. A legendary card can switch to its shiny animation. Burning plays the flame, then the card is gone and the bits are yours.
- **Select** ticks cards instead of opening them. Tag the ones you picked, give them one frame, or burn them together. Bits follow [**/burn**](/commands/burn), including Burn value, whether you pick one card or several. Akira asks before anything is destroyed. A card in an open trade stays put.

## What you cannot do yet

In Discord, [**/collection**](/commands/collection) can filter by series, quality, and print. The WebUI search is a single box, not those filters.
