When you are [signed in](/webui/authentication), **Characters** is the catalog. Click a series to filter the list, then click a character to see that edition's art, how many prints exist, and who holds each one.

## Getting there

1. Open the WebUI link your admin shared (and finish sign-in if asked).
2. On **Home**, click **Characters** - same kind of link as **Collection** and **Economy**.

## What you see

- Series posters use the AniList cover when Akira has one. The ring is how many distinct characters you own in that show. A show at 100% reads COMPLETED.
- Hover a poster to pin it. On a phone the pin stays on the poster. Akira tracks four shows. Pinned shows sit at the top, the same four as on Stats.
- Search finds English titles, Romaji titles, aliases, and character names. Click a series poster to add a filter chip in the search box; the X clears it. [Settings](/webui/settings) picks which title is shown.
- Order defaults to completion: series by percent, characters missing first. Switch the dropdown to Alphabetical or Most recently collected. Recent is the latest card you grabbed in that series, or of that character. Pinned series stay at the top.
- On a character, the portrait shows the name on the art. Beside it is a directory of who holds each quality: Discord picture, name, and a circle with the lowest print they own of that quality. Click a person to open that print in their [collection](/webui/collection). Your copies are tinted. A print sitting on a drop says Unclaimed.
- **Watch this character** is the same list as [**/wishlist**](/commands/wishlist).

The character name on a [collection](/webui/collection) card opens this page for that character.
