---
title: Prestige
description: Reset the milestone bar after step 5 and keep a small permanent percent.
---

Prestige is optional. It opens once you have claimed [milestone 5](/economy/personal-milestone). You can keep climbing instead. There is no frame for prestiging, and prestige does not add bits to [daily](/commands/daily). Cards, bits, tags, and frames stay where they are. The only thing that resets is the milestone cycle, so the 250-bit step is available again.

## What you keep

The percents are not a menu. Prestiging at 5 keeps none. Prestiging later keeps every percent from step 6 through the step you reached.

- After 6: burns pay 1% more.
- After 7: that, and grab cooldown is 1% shorter.
- After 8: those, and drop cooldown is 1% shorter.
- After 9: those, and mints are a little more likely.
- After 10: those, and main characters are 1% more likely.

A later prestige adds another 1% to every buff that prestige included. Two prestiges at step 10 is 2% on all five, not 1% on the last one only.

The 5 minute drop floor and the 1 minute 15 second grab floor only cap the shop and server cooldown buffs. These percents apply on top of that.

## What /prestige tells you

[`/prestige`](/commands/prestige) always says how many bits you still have to give before the next milestone, and what waiting to claim it would add.

- Before step 6, waiting adds the next bit payout. Once step 5 is claimed, it also tells you that continuing to step 6 is the first permanent.
- From step 6 on, waiting adds that step's permanent on top of every earlier one.
- At step 10 there is no next milestone, so it says this is the last one.

When step 5 is already claimed, the message has a confirm button, the same idea as a trade. Confirming sets the bar and the claim count back to 0 and stamps the permanents for every step past 5 that you had claimed. Before step 5 there is no button. The command only shows the distance.

The Economy page uses the same confirm: Prestige, then Confirm prestige, or Keep the bar.
