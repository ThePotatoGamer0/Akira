---
title: Prestige
description: Reset the milestone bar after stage 5 and keep a small permanent percent.
---

Prestige is optional. It opens once you have claimed [milestone 5](/economy/personal-milestone). You can keep climbing instead. Cards, bits, tags, and frames stay where they are. Daily stays the same. The milestone cycle goes back to the 250-bit stage.

## What you keep

The percents are not a menu. Prestiging at 5 keeps none. Prestiging later keeps every percent from stage 6 through the stage you reached.

- After 6: burns pay 1% more.
- After 7: that, and grab cooldown is 1% shorter.
- After 8: those, and drop cooldown is 1% shorter.
- After 9: those, and mints are a little more likely.
- After 10: those, and main characters are 1% more likely.

A later prestige adds another 1% to every buff that prestige included. Two prestiges at stage 10 is 2% on all five, not 1% on the last one only.

The 5 minute drop floor and the 1 minute 15 second grab floor only cap the shop and server cooldown buffs. These percents apply on top of that.

## What /prestige tells you

[`/prestige`](/commands/prestige) always says how many bits you still have to give before the next milestone, and what waiting to claim it would add.

- Before stage 6, waiting adds the next bit payout. Once stage 5 is claimed, it also tells you that continuing to stage 6 is the first permanent.
- From stage 6 on, waiting adds that stage's permanent on top of every earlier one.
- At stage 10 there is no next stage, so it says this is the last one.

When stage 5 is already claimed, the message has a confirm button, the same idea as a trade. Confirming sets the bar and the claim count back to 0 and stamps the permanents for every stage past 5 that you had claimed. Before stage 5 there is no button. The command only shows the distance.

The Economy page uses the same confirm: Prestige, then Confirm prestige, or Keep the bar.
