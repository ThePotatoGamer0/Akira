By using Akira, you agree to these terms. If you run your own copy, you are responsible for how that instance is hosted and who can reach it.

## Who this is for

Akira is meant for people who already share a Discord server and want a cosy card game together. It is not a commercial product, a marketplace, or a paid service.

## Discord's rules still apply

Akira lives inside Discord. You must follow [Discord's Terms of Service](https://discord.com/terms) and [Community Guidelines](https://discord.com/guidelines). If Discord bans an account or a server for breaking those rules, Akira cannot override that.

## Accounts and access

- Playing through Discord uses your Discord account. Akira does not replace Discord login.
- The browser WebUI may ask for a one-time DM code and an optional site password. That password is only for the WebUI. Keep it separate from Discord.
- Server admins control who can invite the bot, where drops happen, and who gets admin tools in the WebUI.

## Fair play

Use Akira the way a friend group would: drop, grab, trade, burn, and chat. Do not:

- Try to break into someone else's account, binder, or admin tools
- Abuse bugs to steal cards, bits, or progress from other players
- Spam the bot, the drop channel, or verification DMs
- Use Akira to harass people or to post content that Discord would not allow

Admins on a given server (and the people who host that instance) may reset progress, remove cards, or restrict access when something goes wrong or someone breaks these rules.

## Virtual items

Cards, bits, tags, buffs, milestones, and prestige are **virtual game items**. They have no cash value. They cannot be redeemed for money, and Akira does not sell real-world goods through the bot.

Akira does not claim ownership of anime or game intellectual property. If you contribute art or data, you confirm you have the right to share it under this project's rules.

Messages you send through the WebUI channel page are posted into Discord as you. The same Discord rules apply there.
